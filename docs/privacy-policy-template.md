# Privacy policy template

> A starting point for the `privacy` page on your Gather instance, not legal advice.
> Replace everything in `[square brackets]`, delete what doesn't apply, and have it
> checked by someone qualified if the details matter where you operate. Publish it
> from **Admin > Pages** with the slug `privacy`; the newsletter signup box and
> emails then link to it automatically.

## Who we are

[Organisation name] runs [site name] at [site address]. We are the controller of the
personal data described below. You can contact us at [contact email or postal address].

## What we collect

If you subscribe to the "What’s on this weekend" email we store:

- your **email address**;
- the **date and time you confirmed** your subscription (our record of your consent);
- random tokens that make your confirmation and unsubscribe links work.

We do not store your IP address or browser details with your subscription.

## Why we collect it and our legal basis

We use your email address only to send you the "What’s on this weekend" email: a short roundup of
events for the coming weekend. We do this **with your consent**, which you give by
confirming your subscription. We do not use it for anything else and we never sell or
share it.

## Who processes it for us

- [Mail provider / SMTP service], which delivers our emails. [Where they process data, e.g. UK, EU, US.]
- [Hosting provider], where this site and its database run. [Location.]
- Aggregate usage statistics (such as which links in the email were clicked and
  roughly how many emails were opened) are collected with [Umami / analytics
  provider]. These are not linked to your email address.

[If any of the above process data outside the UK/EEA, say so here and describe the
safeguards you rely on.]

## How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, your email address is **deleted**. If you
sign up but never confirm, we delete the address after 7 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete
it, and you can withdraw your consent whenever you like by using the unsubscribe link at
the bottom of any email (one click, no login). [If you are in the UK/EEA: you also have
the right to complain to your data protection authority, for example [the ICO / your
national authority].]

## Changes

If we change how we use your data we will update this page. [Last updated: date.]
